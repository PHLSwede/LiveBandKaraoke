NIGHT OF A GIG — STEP BY STEP
BEFORE YOU START
OPEN BAND DASHBOARD
Go to purplesandwich.netlify.app/band on the laptop. This is your control centre for the night.
CREATE AN EVENT
Events tab → fill in the name, venue and date → Create & Go Live. This activates the singer portal.
SET UP THE SCREENS
Open /stage in a separate window → AirPlay to TV → fullscreen. Open /board on iPad → fullscreen → leave on the bar.
SHARE THE QR CODE
The Queue Board shows a QR code automatically. Singers scan it to reach the portal. You can also print one pointing to /sing.
RUNNING THE NIGHT
SINGERS REQUEST
Singers submit up to 3 song picks from their phone. They land in your Bullpen in order of submission.
ADD TO QUEUE
Queue tab → Bullpen. Pick which of their 3 songs to use, hit Add to Queue. They're removed from the bullpen and added to the queue. Singer's phone updates automatically.
HIT PLAY
Queue tab → Queue. Hit ▶ Play on the next singer. Their chord sheet loads on the TV instantly. Singer's phone shows "GET UP THERE!"
MARK COMPLETE
When they're done, hit ✓ Complete. The song is logged to history, the queue advances, and the singer's phone shows "Great Job!"
END OF NIGHT
Events tab → Close Night. The setlist is saved. You can export it as plain text any time from the History tab.

UNDER THE HOOD
Built with React, hosted on Netlify (free tier), database on Supabase (free tier). Singer requests sync across all devices in real time — no refresh needed. Everything runs in the browser, nothing to install.
